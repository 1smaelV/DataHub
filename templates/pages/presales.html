Discovery Checklist
QUALIFICATION

Customer Discovery

Identify the customer's current data stack, pain points, volume of data, and existing cloud provider. Map their use cases to Databricks capabilities.

STEP 1 · DISCOVERY

Use Case Mapping

Match identified business problems to Databricks solutions: ETL modernization, ML platform, real-time analytics, data governance with Unity Catalog.

STEP 2 · MAPPING

Value Proposition

Articulate ROI: reduced infrastructure cost, faster time-to-insight, unified platform replacing multiple point solutions, and open-source compatibility.

STEP 3 · PITCH

Qualification Criteria

Assess budget, timeline, decision-maker access, data maturity level and technical readiness before investing in a full POV or demo build.

STEP 4 · QUALIFY
