About DataHub
Internal Knowledge Portal, Technical Architecture & Governance Framework
DataHub v3 is the centralized Databricks enablement and acceleration platform maintained by the Cloud Data & AI Center of Excellence (CoE). For questions, architecture reviews, or contributions, visit our internal channel at #databricks-coe.

Mission & Purpose

Empower engineering, pre-sales, and delivery teams with standardized playbooks, reusable code patterns, and architectural blueprints for client engagements.

CORE · STRATEGY

Assets & Accelerators

Pre-packaged Medallion Lakehouse templates, cluster sizing calculators, Delta Live Tables pipelines, and infrastructure as code bundles.

ACCELERATION · ASSETS

Governance & Quality

Enforce Unity Catalog security policies, CI/CD deployment checklists, automated tests, and mandatory architecture reviews prior to production.
