Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Answers to common questions about Databricks, environments, and internal workflows
Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out on Slack at #databricks-coe or consult the Glossary.
How do I request access to a Databricks Workspace? ▼
Access requests are handled through our internal Identity & Access Management portal. Submit a ticket requesting the appropriate role (Developer, Data Analyst, or Data Engineer) for your specific project cluster.
What is the difference between Serverless and Classic Compute? ▼
Serverless compute provides instant startup (seconds instead of minutes), zero infrastructure management, and automatic elasticity managed by Databricks. Classic compute runs on dedicated VMs in our cloud tenant and requires sizing and warm-up time.
How should I organize tables in Unity Catalog? ▼
Follow the 3-level namespace: catalog.schema.table. Typically: [environment]_[domain].[layer].[entity], for example: prod_analytics.silver.sales_orders.
What is the deployment procedure for Databricks Apps? ▼
Use the Databricks CLI v0.200+: first synchronize your code with databricks sync --watch . <workspace_path>, then deploy with databricks apps deploy <app_name> --source-code-path <workspace_path>.
Where can I get vouchers for Databricks Certifications? ▼
Visit the Certifications tab to learn about our corporate enablement roadmap and apply for exam vouchers supported by the CoE.
