Proof of Value (POV)
Structured approach to validating Databricks in the customer environment
ℹ️ A POV is a time-boxed (typically 2–4 weeks) technical engagement to prove Databricks solves the customer's specific use case with their own data.
POV Framework
4-WEEK MODEL

Week 1 — Define Success Criteria

Co-define 3–5 measurable success criteria with the customer. Examples: pipeline runs in under 10 min, data quality score above 98%, cost reduction of 30% vs current solution.

PLANNING

Week 2 — Environment & Data Prep

Provision POV workspace, ingest sample customer data into Bronze layer, validate connectivity to source systems, establish baseline metrics.

SETUP

Week 3 — Build & Validate

Implement the agreed use case end-to-end. Run validation against the success criteria. Collect performance benchmarks and stakeholder feedback.

BUILD

Week 4 — Readout & Next Steps

Present results to all stakeholders. Map POV findings to a full delivery scope. Produce a POV Report documenting success, gaps, and recommended architecture.
