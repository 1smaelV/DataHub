What is Databricks?
Introduction to the platform and its role in our organization
ℹ️ Databricks is the primary data and AI platform we use at the company for data engineering, ML and unified analytics.
The platform

Databricks is a cloud-based data analytics and machine learning platform built on Apache Spark. It unifies data engineering, data science and business analytics in a single collaborative environment.

We use it to process large volumes of data, build automated ETL pipelines and train ML models that power our products.
